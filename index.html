ABOUT PR IMMIGRATION

Your goals. Your journey. Our guidance.

PR IMMIGRATION is an education and immigration centre in Karnal offering English-language preparation and visa-related guidance for students, travellers and aspiring international applicants.

Our services include IELTS, PTE and Spoken English training along with Study Visa and Tourist Visa assistance. We focus on clear communication, organised guidance and helping applicants understand their next steps.
